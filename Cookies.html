Cookies Policy

This policy explains how VibeRoomz may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your website experience.

Last updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in your browser. They can help websites remember preferences, maintain sessions and understand how visitors interact with the website.

VibeRoomz may use cookies and similar technologies to provide a smoother, more reliable and personalized experience.

2. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies may be used for several purposes across the VibeRoomz website, including:

  • Remembering website and account preferences
  • Maintaining login and session information
  • Supporting booking and reservation functionality
  • Improving website performance
  • Understanding general website usage
  • Providing a more consistent user experience

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Different cookies may serve different purposes. The categories below describe common cookie types that may be used by VibeRoomz or its service providers.

Essential Cookies

These cookies may be necessary for core website functionality, account sessions, security and booking-related features.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may remember choices such as language, display preferences or other settings to make future visits more convenient.

Analytics Cookies

Where enabled, analytics technologies may help us understand general traffic and how visitors use different areas of the website.

Third-Party Cookies

Certain external services integrated into the website may place or access cookies according to their own policies.

4. Cookies & Your Account

If you sign in to a VibeRoomz account, cookies or similar technologies may be used to maintain your session and remember account-related preferences.

This can help you move between pages without needing to repeatedly authenticate during an active session.

5. Cookies & Bookings

Cookies or session technologies may support parts of the room reservation process, such as maintaining temporary booking information and keeping your session active while you complete a reservation.

Disabling essential technologies may prevent certain booking or account features from working correctly.

6. Analytics & Website Improvement

Where analytics tools are enabled, information about general website activity may help VibeRoomz understand which pages and features are useful to visitors.

Analytics information may be used to identify usability issues, improve page performance and make the website easier to navigate.

7. Third-Party Services

Some VibeRoomz features may rely on third-party services, such as payment providers, analytics platforms, embedded content or other technical services.

These third parties may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

8. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to control, block or delete cookies through browser settings.

  • Allow or block cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Clear website data
  • Choose how long certain data is retained

Browser settings differ between devices and browsers. Blocking essential cookies may affect some VibeRoomz features.

9. Cookie Security

Where cookies are used for authentication or other sensitive purposes, appropriate security controls should be applied by the underlying website and server systems.

Cookies should not be treated as a secure place to store sensitive information such as passwords or complete payment-card details.

10. Local Storage & Similar Technologies

In addition to traditional cookies, VibeRoomz may use browser storage technologies such as local storage or session storage for certain interface preferences and application functionality.

These technologies work differently from cookies and may be controlled separately through browser settings.

11. Changes to This Cookies Policy

VibeRoomz may update this Cookies Policy when our website, technologies or services change.

Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised update date.
