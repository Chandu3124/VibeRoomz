1. Introduction

Welcome to VibeRoomz. This Privacy Policy explains how VibeRoomz collects, uses, stores and protects information when you browse our website, create an account, make a booking or contact our team.

By using our website and services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you use VibeRoomz, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

  • Full name and contact details

  • Account login information
  • Room booking date and preferred time
  • Number of guests and booking preferences
  • Payment and billing information when applicable
  • Messages or information submitted through support forms

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through VibeRoomz may be used to:

  • Create and manage your customer account
  • Process and manage room reservations
  • Send booking confirmations and updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Process payments and billing requests
  • Improve our website and customer experience

VibeRoomz does not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with trusted service providers only when required to operate our services, process payments, provide technical support or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Third-party service providers are expected to handle information only for the purposes necessary to provide their services.

Payment information may be processed through authorized payment service providers. VibeRoomz does not intentionally store complete payment-card credentials on its own systems when a third-party payment processor is used.

Payment providers may have their own privacy policies and security practices that apply to transactions processed through their platforms.

6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

VibeRoomz may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain sessions, improve website functionality and understand general website usage.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

7. Data Security

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may also choose not to receive promotional communication by using the available unsubscribe option or contacting us.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.
